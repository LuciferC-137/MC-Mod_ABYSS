The Abyss is a dark and mysterious place, filled with ancient ruins of a long-gone civilization.
The player will have to explore the Abyss to find new resources,
new mobs, new structures, and defeat the Contagion!

This mod adds a new dimension revolving around the ancient city ambiance, adding unique lore and
gameplay mechanics. The new dimension Abyss is intended to be reached after the End to offer the player
a new challenge and a new experience. The Abyss is a dark and mysterious place, filled with ancient ruins
of a long-gone civilization. Follow the guidance of the Wind to conquer the six Crystal Temples and challenge the great Contagion Incarnation in its Ancient Citadel !

Where does the corruption come from ? What is the Radiance that seems to fight the darkness ?

Will you choose to fight the darkness or to become a part of it ?

Venture into a dark new dimension born of a fallen Ancient Civilization,
uncover the deep secrets lurking beneath its shadows, and brave the Temples,
the Ancient City, and its Citadel as you choose whether to resist the Corruption… or join it.
